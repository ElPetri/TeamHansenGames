The Simplest Strategy

Warren Buffett has repeatedly recommended that most people simply invest in a low-cost S&P 500 index fund. VOO (Vanguard S&P 500 ETF, expense ratio: 0.03%) and SPY are popular choices. You don't need to pick stocks — just buy the whole market and hold it. Time in the market beats timing the market, every time.
